How it works

Orbis Generator creates redeem codes for PS4 homebrew packages hosted on GitHub.

01
Paste a URL

Enter a GitHub Release page URL or a direct .pkg download link into the field.

02
Choose a type

Free codes are valid for 24 h. Premium codes require watching 2 short ads.

03
Redeem on PS4

Go to the Redeem tab, enter the 6-character code in the Orbis Store app on your PS4.

Orbis is open-source — contribute on GitHub.
